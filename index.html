The Hollow Meridian

A lighthouse-observatory at the edge of the Sound · 1889

The boatman left you in the flooded antechamber and rowed away without a word. Somewhere above you is a meridian instrument that was sealed on the sixth of March and never opened since. Everything you need is written down here — if you can find it, and if you can read it.
